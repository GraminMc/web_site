MINECRAFT SERVER RULES

Please follow these guidelines strictly to keep the community safe and fair for everyone.

1

No Hacking or Cheating

Using hacked clients, X-Ray, Fly, Kill Aura, Auto Clicker, or any other unfair advantage is strictly prohibited.

2

Respect Everyone

Treat all players with respect. Bullying, harassment, discrimination, or toxic behavior is not allowed.

3

No Spamming

Do not spam messages, commands, advertisements, or repeated characters in the server chat.

4

No Inappropriate Content

Do not share inappropriate, offensive, NSFW, or disturbing content in chat, builds, skins, or usernames.

5

No Griefing

Do not destroy, modify, burn, or damage another player's builds or property without permission.

6

No Stealing

Taking items, blocks, pets, or resources from another player's base or storage without permission is prohibited.

7

No Unwanted PvP

Do not attack or kill other players without their permission, especially in non-PvP areas.

8

No Bug Exploiting

Do not use server bugs, glitches, duplication exploits, or unintended mechanics to gain an unfair advantage.

9

No Scamming

Do not trick or deceive players during trades, deals, or transactions. Keep all trades fair and honest.

10

Follow Staff Instructions

Respect all server staff and follow their instructions. If you disagree with a decision, contact staff calmly.
