Server Rules

Please adhere to our community guidelines to keep the server fair and fun for everyone

1

No Hacking or Cheating

Use of hacked clients, X-Ray, auto-clickers, or exploits that give an unfair advantage is strictly prohibited and results in a permanent ban.

2

No Griefing or Stealing

Respect other players' builds and land claims. Stealing or destroying claimed/unclaimed player property is not tolerated.

3

Respectful Communication

Keep chat clean and respectful. Toxic behavior, harassment, excessive spam, hate speech, and toxicity will lead to chat mutes or bans.

4

No Advertising

Promoting other Minecraft servers, Discord links, or external commercial links in public chat or private messages is strictly forbidden.
